#### 📚 Notes for Students Seeking Assistance
It's my pleasure to help students in the courses I am responsible for, and I will try my best to answer your questions or make suggestions for your course projects.🤓 **Please feel free to reach out to me by email at any time, as long as your questions are asked in good faith and do not concern matters I cannot address** (such as grading policies, exam questions, or assignment solutions).

**For courses where I am not fully responsible, please contact your assigned TA first.**😍 If you are unable to reach them, you may contact me, but I can only help you get in touch with your TA.
