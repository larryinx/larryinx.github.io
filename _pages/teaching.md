#### 📚 Notes for Students Seeking Assistance
It's my pleasure to help students in the courses I am responsible for, and I will try my best to answer your questions. If I am unable to help, I apologize in advance. I am also very willing to discuss which aspects of the course or possible directions (such as project topics) might benefit your future career or research. Please feel free to reach out to me by email at any time, as long as your questions are asked in good faith and do not concern matters I cannot address (such as grading policies, exam questions, or assignment solutions).

For courses where I am not fully responsible, please contact your assigned TA first. If you are unable to reach them, you may contact me, but I can only assist with academic questions or help you get in touch with your TA.
